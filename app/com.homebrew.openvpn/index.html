How to use OpenVPN

    
    
      
  1. Add your profile. Choose + Add profile. On your phone or computer (same home network) open the address
        shown on the TV, or scan the QR code, enter the 6-digit code and pick your .ovpn file. You can type your VPN
        username and password there too. Or put the .ovpn file on a USB stick and choose Scan USB.
    2. 
      
  2. Connect. Select the profile and press Connect. If the profile needs a login and none is saved, the TV asks
        for it; tick Remember on this TV to save it.
    3. 
      
  3. Watch TV. Once connected, press the Home button and use your other apps. The VPN keeps running in the
        background; Back does not close the app while connected.
    4. 
      
  4. Keep connected reconnects automatically if the connection drops and after the TV restarts (needs a saved login).
    5. 
      
  5. Local network access keeps your home devices able to reach the TV (casting, remote apps) while the VPN is on.
    6. 
      
  6. Connection details shows the server, encryption, VPN address, routes, DNS, speed and traffic. Log shows
        OpenVPN's own messages if something goes wrong.
    7. 
      
  7. Disconnect stops the VPN (and Keep connected won't bring it back until you connect again).
    8. 
    

    
Needs a rooted LG TV with Homebrew Channel. Profiles must contain their certificates inline (one .ovpn file).
      Scripts inside profiles are removed for safety.
